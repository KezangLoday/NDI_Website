— Company

We build the trust layer for a digital Bhutan

Bhutan NDI is a Druk Holding & Investments company delivering the world's first national digital identity built entirely on decentralized, self-sovereign identity technology.

A national identity designed around the citizen

Bhutan NDI began with a question: what if identity infrastructure gave people more control instead of less? The answer was a decentralized architecture — credentials issued by trusted institutions, held by the citizen, verified cryptographically by any service.

We work with government agencies, banks, universities and private companies to connect real services to that infrastructure, and with international partners to keep it interoperable beyond our borders.
